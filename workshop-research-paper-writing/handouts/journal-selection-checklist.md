# Journal Selection Checklist

Complete for **three** candidate journals before you start writing, then format for the target from the first draft.

---

## Part A · Verification sequence (10 minutes per journal, always in this order)

Never start from an email. Always start from the publisher's own domain.

| # | Check | Where | J1 | J2 | J3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Journal found on the **publisher's own site** | ieee.org, springer.com, elsevier.com, acm.org, wiley.com, mdpi.com, tandfonline.com … | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 2 | Listed in **Web of Science Core Collection**; note the exact index (SCIE / SSCI / AHCI / ESCI) | `mjl.clarivate.com` | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 3 | **Active** Scopus coverage (not discontinued); note CiteScore | `scopus.com/sources` or the Scopus Source List file | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 4 | Quartile + SJR + **subject category** | `scimagojr.com` | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 5 | If open access: listed in **DOAJ** | `doaj.org` | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 6 | **ISSN** matches this journal (catches hijacked clones) | `portal.issn.org` | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 7 | Publisher/journal is a **COPE** member | `publicationethics.org` | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 8 | **3 editorial board members verified** on their institutional pages | institution websites | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 9 | Read **2 recent papers** — is this the standard you want to be judged by? | journal site | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |
| 10 | Not a hijacked/cloned title | Retraction Watch hijacked-journal checker; Think.Check.Submit | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ |

## Part C · Predatory / low-integrity red flags

Tick any that apply. **One tick is a reason to investigate; two or more is a reason to walk away.**

☐ Unsolicited flattering email invited the submission
☐ Promises publication in days, or "guaranteed acceptance"
☐ Advertises an unrecognised metric ("Global Impact Factor", "Universal Impact Factor", "Journal Impact Score")
☐ Claims "Scopus indexed" but fails check 3 above
☐ Claims "SCI indexed" but is absent from the Master Journal List
☐ Impossibly broad scope spanning unrelated disciplines
☐ APC not disclosed before acceptance, or payment to a personal account
☐ Editorial board without affiliations, or members who did not consent
☐ Website closely imitates a well-known journal at a slightly different URL
☐ No DOIs assigned; no archiving/preservation policy
☐ Contact address is a free email account
☐ Grammatical errors on the journal's own homepage
☐ Rapid "review" with no substantive reviewer comments

**Consequences of publishing in a predatory venue:** the work may be uncitable, may be rejected for degree requirements, generally cannot be withdrawn and republished elsewhere, and the APC is unrecoverable.
